# mon-restaurant.eu

- What it is: an all-in-one, commission-free online tool for restaurants: the restaurant's own website with online table booking (with deposits), takeaway and delivery ordering paid online, floor plan and table service with payment, an online menu imported from a photo, review requests and AI-drafted replies to Google reviews, email/SMS campaigns, and a dashboard with a customer file.
- Who it is for: independent restaurants in Belgium (French-speaking customer experience), open and already trading, whose owner wants to take bookings or orders online without paying platform commissions.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription, list price 197 € / month excl. VAT; launch offer 98.50 € / month excl. VAT locked for life for the first 20 restaurants; no commission, no commitment, everything included; 250 SMS / month included then 0.08 € per SMS; card-payment provider fees extra (paid by the restaurant to its own payment account). Beta: 3 "founding" restaurants get it free for life in exchange for real use, weekly feedback, three meetings and a testimonial.
- Where and how: online SaaS (mon-restaurant.eu), sold in Belgium; leads come from the website (call-back form or a booked 30-minute call); a 6-step guided setup, done for the restaurant during the beta. The restaurant delivers its own orders; payments go straight to the restaurant's own payment account.
- Budget and constraints: run by Chacun Son Job SComm. Budget, team size, time available and the state of the product (live customers, how much is built) are not stated: open questions.
