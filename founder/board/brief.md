# Board brief: mon-restaurant.eu

Everything below is what the founder's public website (mon-restaurant.eu and mon-restaurant.eu/beta/) says, read on 2026-11-15. Nothing here is verified beyond that. Anything not listed is unknown.

## The business
- Product: mon-restaurant.eu, "un service de Chacun Son Job SComm" (a Belgian cooperative company). SaaS for restaurants.
- Headline: "Votre salle pleine. Vos commandes en direct. Zéro commission." Customers book, order and pay on the restaurant's own site "pendant que vous, vous cuisinez".
- Pains it names: the phone ringing mid-service and lost bookings; delivery platforms taking a commission on every order and keeping the customer; no-shows (e.g. a table of 8 on a Saturday); an out-of-date online menu.

## What is included (one plan, nothing optional)
1. Bookings: 24/7 on real tables, deposits (per person or per table, for everyone or only large groups, certain days/hours), automatic refund up to a chosen deadline, e-mail reminder the day before and SMS 2 h before, self-service change/cancel, slot spacing, closures, private-room blocking.
2. Takeaway & delivery: own online ordering, paid upfront, scheduled orders, delivery zones by km with fees/minimums/free threshold, kitchen alert, customer status updates. The restaurant's own staff deliver.
3. Table service: floor plan editor, live table status, walk-ins, order-taking at the table sent to the kitchen, payment by card/cash/Bancontact at terminal/bank transfer noted, tips, split bills, QR code menu per table.
4. Menu: import from photo, PDF, Excel or existing site; sizes, extras, daily stock, sold-out auto-reset, time-based categories.
5. Website: ready once menu and hours are entered, French-language, three styles, own domain name, e-mail-code customer login, Meta pixel.
6. Reviews & loyalty: automatic review requests, AI-drafted replies to Google reviews, e-mail and SMS campaigns (250 SMS/month included, then 0.08 €).
7. Management: dashboard (orders, net sales, online bookings), customer cards with visit history, team alerts.
- Setup: six guided steps; test mode before going live.

## Price
- List price 197 € / month excl. VAT. Launch: -50 % for life (98.50 € / month excl. VAT) for the first 20 restaurants. No commission, no commitment, "quel que soit votre volume".
- Card-payment provider fees not included (the restaurant connects its own payment account; money goes straight to the restaurant).
- The site's calculator compares against platform commissions, e.g. "Soit jusqu'à 16 818 € gardés chaque année" (indicative).

## Go-to-market
- Website with a call-back form or a booked 30-minute call.
- Beta programme: 3 founding restaurants, free during the beta (max 3 months) and free for life afterwards, with done-for-you setup and a WhatsApp line. In exchange: go live with online bookings or ordering within 30 days, three 45-minute meetings, weekly 2-minute feedback, report bugs, a testimonial if satisfied. If the commitment lapses, they move to 98.50 € / month with one month's notice.
- Who can apply: an independent restaurant in Belgium, open and trading, whose owner has a little time, and who wants online bookings or orders.

## Unknown (do not assume)
- Number of current customers (the site shows only illustrative examples: "restaurant et chiffres fictifs").
- Founder team, technical capacity, how much of the product is built and in production.
- Budget, runway, cost to serve per restaurant (hosting, SMS, support, AI).
- Payment provider used, cost of acquiring a restaurant, churn.
- Competitors (not named on the site beyond "plateformes de livraison" and "téléphone et carnet").
